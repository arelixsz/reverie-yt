Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Reverie YT is a private tool used only by its owner, for the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

  • Permission to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's channel (youtube.upload).
  • Read-only access to the owner's channel and video information (youtube.readonly).

It cannot delete or edit existing videos, comments or playlists, and has no access to payments or account settings.

How data is used and stored

Data from Google is used only to upload the owner's videos and confirm they were published. The access token is stored on the owner's private server, readable only by the owner's software. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Reverie YT's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored token is then useless and is deleted.
